Every correct answer earns XP

Correct answers earn XP when shooting and saving. A win multiplies the XP from that shootout. A perfect score is not required.

Daily streak

{% for day in ['Mon','Tue','Wed','Thu','Fri','Sat','Sun'] %}{{ day }}{% endfor %}

Finish a shootout and answer every question before time runs out. Winning is not required. A timeout or ending early does not extend the streak. Each day resets at 00:00 UTC.

Fresh questions

Registered players do not receive the same question again within a week. The question pool resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Choose Easy, Medium or Hard. Every new shootout mixes teams and topics at your chosen difficulty. Questions and answer options are shuffled.

Guest or registered?

Guest games are unlimited. An account saves XP, levels, streaks and your match record. Guest results stay separate and do not transfer to a new account.

Your team, your badge

your_usernameExample identity

Joining a team adds its badge beside your name on your profile and the player leaderboard. It is an optional way to show which club you support.

Club identity

Your team gives no XP bonus and does not change your questions or difficulty. Your XP and record stay yours. After joining, switching or leaving a team, you must wait 3 days before changing again.

Any club, every game

Everyone plays from the mixed question pool at their chosen difficulty. Supporting Manchester United does not limit your questions to Manchester United.

The team leaderboard adds up current members' XP, levels, wins and current streaks. Team win rate is the average of members' win rates; members with no recorded win or loss in the selected period are excluded from that average. When you change teams, your stats move with you. XP, wins and win rate can be filtered by period; levels and current streaks are not limited to that period.
