Daily Challenge

You only shoot and have three lives. Score as many goals as you can. Each wrong answer or timeout costs a life.

Only your first attempt each day counts, even if you quit. Later games are practice. The leaderboard tracks your best score ever. Your daily attempt resets at midnight UTC.

Questions mix all difficulties. Correct answers earn XP, even in practice, with no win bonus. You need an account to play.

Play Daily Challenge

Challenge a friend

Choose a difficulty and create a duel. Send your friend the invite link, then both press Ready to start. No account is needed. Duels give no XP or rating.

You both answer the same question, one as the shooter and one as the keeper. If both answers are correct, the faster answer decides the penalty.

Your team, your look

The taker wears your kit when you shoot. The goalkeeper wears it when you save. In Friend Duels, each player wears their own choice.

You contribute your stats to the team leaderboard. Your club gives no gameplay bonuses. You can change teams once every 3 days using Change team. Changing teams switches you to the new club's home kit. Your shirt number stays the same.
