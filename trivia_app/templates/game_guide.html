Every correct answer earns XP

Correct answers earn XP when shooting and saving. A win multiplies the XP from that shootout. A perfect score is not required.

Daily streak

Finish a shootout and answer every question before time runs out. Winning is not required. A timeout or ending early does not extend the streak. Each day resets at 00:00 UTC.

Fresh questions

Registered players do not receive the same question again within a week. The question pool resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Choose Easy, Medium or Hard. Every new shootout mixes teams and topics at your chosen difficulty. Questions and answer options are shuffled.

Guest or registered?

Guest Solo games and Friend Duels are open to everyone. An account saves XP, levels, streaks and your match record, unlocks Daily Challenge and lets you add friends. Guest results stay separate and do not transfer to a new account.

Daily Challenge

Registered players share a new pool at 00:00 UTC: 10 Easy, 20 Medium and 20 Hard questions. Play the usual shootout, not a fixed 50-question quiz. Correct answers earn 10, 20 or 30 XP, without a win multiplier.

Win the shootout or use the remaining daily pool before a winner is decided to earn one Daily win. An already decided loss stays a loss. An empty pool before starting never awards a win. You can earn at most one Daily win per UTC day.

Only questions you actually open are excluded from later games until Monday's weekly reset. Unopened questions stay available. Daily wins have their own leaderboard and do not count as Solo wins.

Challenge a friend

Create an invitation and send the link to a friend. Neither player needs an account. Choose a difficulty and get ready together. Friend Duels award no XP, rating or streak credit and never change Solo statistics.

You both receive the same question and have 15 seconds. Correct answers less than 150 ms apart also replay the kick. Replays do not use a penalty or switch roles. Server receipt time decides speed.

Five penalties each, early clinches and paired sudden death. Leaving forfeits the duel. A player missing two answers in a row, or disconnected for over 30 seconds between kicks, forfeits. If both players are absent, the match ends without a winner. Sessions expire after 30 minutes.

Your team, your badge

your_usernameExample identity

Joining a team adds its badge beside your name on your profile and the player leaderboard. It is an optional way to show which club you support.

Club identity

Your team gives no XP bonus and does not change your questions or difficulty. Your XP and record stay yours. After joining, switching or leaving a team, you must wait 3 days before changing again.

Any club, every game

Everyone plays from the mixed question pool at their chosen difficulty. Supporting Manchester United does not limit your questions to Manchester United.

The team leaderboard adds up current members' XP, levels, wins and current streaks. Team win rate is the average of members' win rates; members with no recorded win or loss in the selected period are excluded from that average. When you change teams, your stats move with you. XP, wins and win rate can be filtered by period; levels and current streaks are not limited to that period.
